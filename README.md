# Studio

A small set of story makers: pick a tool, add photos, choose the words, save a full-quality 2160×3840 PNG (lossless, same on iPhone and Android).
Installable as an app (Add to Home Screen on iPhone, Install on Android).
Built so any brand can plug in. The first brand kit is **1st Choice**.

## Tools
### Create
The tool that can make anything: its own simple editor made for phones (one page on screen at a time).
- **Selecting:** tap a thing to select it; tap empty space or **✓** to deselect. Handles are finger-sized: drag a corner to resize, the top circle to turn. Two fingers on a selected thing size and turn it; two fingers elsewhere zoom. Double-tap to change words or crop a photo.
- **Several at once:** drag across empty space to box-select, long-press a thing to add it, or **Select several** in the edit bar (then tap things). On a computer, Shift-click. Then Align, Group, Colour, Duplicate or Delete them together.
- **Edit bar** (above the bottom bar): Words, Colour (brand palette + custom), Font, Size (size, turn, opacity, corners), Arrange (align, layer order, flip), Duplicate, Delete, More (lock, hide, group/ungroup, fill the page). With nothing selected: Page, Background, Select several, Select all.
- **Start:** "What are you making?" Blank Story (1080×1920), Post (1080×1350), Square (1080×1080), Carousel (3 slides at 1080×1350, add more with +) or Highlight cover (square, with the circle Instagram keeps shown as a dashed guide). Or pick a template.
- **Templates:** 29 ready layouts (8 new in 1.0.0: Restock post, Both flavours, This or that, One word post, Map in bio post, Good morning, Highlight: order, Highlight: new), white look (Bingo and Occasion one word are cream). The words come only from approved phrases; spots marked «اكتب هنا» are for your own words. A template can replace the design or be added as new pages.
- **Add:** Elements (button, map-in-bio pill, place + pin, Instagram handle, flavour pills, offer tile, dates line, logo, chat bubbles, typing dots, number circle, dividers and more), Brand (logo, HD cans), Text, Photos & files (also SVG and PDF), Shapes, Lines, Icons, Emoji, Barcode & QR, Frame sizes. Elements are normal layers grouped together. Pills and bubbles re-fit when you change the words (double-tap or the Words button). Ungroup to edit one part.
- **Photo spots:** tap a spot, then Add photo (or the Photo button). The photo takes the spot's size and rounded corners, then crop, zoom or replace it like any photo. Several photos fill the empty spots in order.
- **Pages:** the strip above the edit bar jumps between pages; + adds one after the current page; tap the current page's number (or Page) to change its size, copy it as a story/post/square, duplicate, move or delete it.
- **Save:** full-quality 2× PNG (2160×3840 for a story) of every page you tick. Save to the team too, or **Save as a team template** (words and layout; your photos become empty photo spots). Team templates show in Templates for everyone on the team. The PNG falls back to 1.5× or 1× with a message only if the phone runs out of memory.
- **Drafts:** an unfinished design waits in Continue editing on the hub, one per design. A design saved to the team leaves that list. An unfinished story is kept even if you leave the page straight away (or tap Skip) a moment after a change.

The bottom bar has **Add, Text, Photo, Templates, Layers, Save** on phone and computer. Sheets open from the bottom on a phone and as a panel on the right on a computer; the page moves so a sheet never covers it. Layers lists the page's layers (top first) with hide and lock.

### Video editor
Makes a video from your own clips and photos, text and sound, or from a reel template of the brand kit (`brands/<brand>/reels/<id>/`, formats in `docs/reels.md`), and saves an MP4 (1080×1920, with sound). It opens the first reel template ready to edit. Phone first: a big 9:16 preview, play/pause, and a timeline with a fixed playhead in the middle; scroll the timeline to scrub (pinch or the zoom buttons change the scale). Three lanes: **Clips**, **Text** and **Audio**.
- **Add:** clips and photos from this phone (they go after the clip under the playhead), a reel template, or music and sound (placed at the playhead). In an empty video (**New video** in the name menu) one button starts you off.
- **Select** a block on the timeline: it gets handles to trim it, and the bar below becomes that block's tools. Clips: **Split** at the playhead, **Volume**, **Length** and slow **Zoom** (photos), **Words** and **Mix** (reel templates), **Earlier / Later**, **Delete** (with Undo). Text and sound bars: drag to move in time. Hold a clip and drag to reorder it.
- **Text:** the template's words first (Arabic or English, right to left where it should be; **Use the original words**, **Pick a phrase**), then **Add text**: a layer with its own words, font (Headline/Body), size, colour, a box behind it, in/out animations and exact start/end. Tap a text on the preview to select it, drag to move it (it snaps to the middle lines), double-tap to edit.
- **Sound:** your sounds (mute, volume, **Fades**) and, for each reel template in the video, its mix (volume and mute per sound, **Duck the music under big sounds**, **Reset to original mix**, play-alone).
- **Safe zones:** the icon in the preview's corner shades what Instagram covers. It is never in the saved video.
- **Save:** **Save video** makes the MP4 on your device (progress shown; Chrome and Safari on iOS 17+ make it fast, other browsers in real time and it may stutter with clips). **Save edits to the team** keeps the project's settings as a Saved design. Your clips, photos and sounds stay on the phone that added them; on another device a clear "Missing on this device" block with **Add again** appears. Add `?debug=1` to the address for a small panel with what loaded, to send a screenshot when a preview stays blank.

## On a phone
The preview fills the screen. The bottom bar has **Photo, Words, Style, Layers, Save**; each opens a sheet over the preview so you see every change.
**Save** opens a small Save sheet, ready to go: **Save to the team** is on when the phone is signed in to the team (for a new design; a design opened from Saved is only updated in the team when you switch it on), the name is filled in (tool + date) and the collection is the one used in the last team save (`studio:lastCols:<brand>`; a collection that no longer exists is dropped). One tap on **Save & add to team** makes the full-quality PNG (share sheet: choose “Save Image”) and saves the design to the team; **More options** has Keep the photos and Save as a new design. **Only save to the team** skips the picture. After a Quick start, a small **Your photo is in** note shows over the bottom of the design for about 3 seconds (swipe it down or tap the photo to hide it sooner) while Save softly pulses; until you change something, **Save** on the bar does it all in one tap (picture + team), so Restock from a photo is two taps: the camera button, then Save. Edit the story after saving and the next Save makes a fresh picture. On a computer the same panels sit beside the preview and Save saves in one click. On a computer the Save card sits at the end of the steps; while it is out of sight, a slim bar at the bottom of the steps has the same Save button (one click brings the card up and saves).

**Search** (top of the hub): one box finds everything in Studio: tools, templates (the brand's Create templates and team templates), saved designs and collections, unfinished stories, and settings and actions (All tools, Saved designs, Sign in / Account, What's new, Install Studio, list or grid, Experimental). Arabic matches with or without harakat and alef/teh marbuta forms. Templates show their real preview in their own shape (stories tall, posts 4:5, squares, round highlight covers; 1.7.0). Before you type it shows your last 6 searches (kept on this phone) and shortcuts (the first pinned tool from a photo, Continue, All tools, Templates, Saved designs, What's new). On a phone it opens full screen and Back closes it; on a computer it is a dropdown (arrows + Enter, Esc). The magnifier sits at the start of the box and its own clear ✕ at the end; a result whose name is Arabic shows right to left (picture, words and arrow, `dir="auto"` on the row). Built in `assets/search.js/.css`; the hub hands it the data (`searchEntries` in `home()`). Create templates are found by their Arabic words too (1.11.0); a hit builds the template on a fresh Create, or opens the picker on it over existing work.

**Saved cards** (1.6.0): an automatic name (tool + date, e.g. “Restock – 24 Oct”) shows just the tool's name on the card when that date is the day the card's caption gives, so it fits on one line; the full name stays in the tooltip, the menu, Peek and search. Typed names are never shortened. Captions for designs older than a week read “18 Oct” (`dayTag()`), never the phone's date format.

**Your tools** (hub): only the tools pinned on this phone. **All tools** shows your pinned tools first (**On your home**, in home order; press and drag the dots at the end of a row to reorder, or the arrow keys on a keyboard), then every other tool in its group, each once, with a star to pin or unpin it (also the star in search results). Pins live in this phone's storage (`studio:pins`), not with the team login. A new phone starts with Restock; a phone that used Studio before 0.6.0 keeps Restock, Photo story and Collage. When photos are still waiting for Restock or Photo story, the tool's row in Your tools and All tools shows a small "N waiting" tag; it goes when the photos are done or cleared with ✕. Since 1.22.0 tapping a row with that tag goes straight on with the next waiting photo (like the **N photos still waiting** row), and the tool no longer shows a second "unfinished story" banner over it.

**+ button** (bottom corner of the hub, beside the bottom bar on a phone): a small menu with **Restock**, **Photo story** and **Collage** from a photo (each with its own icon), then **New design** (a blank page in Create) and a small **More tools** that opens All tools (Carousel and Highlight covers live there; Templates has its own tab). **Start from a photo**: pinned Restock, Photo story and Collage rows also have a camera button, one tap to the phone's camera / photos. Take or pick the photo(s) and the tool opens with them already in (Collage takes 2–9 and sets the number of boxes; Carousel takes up to 10 and Highlight covers up to 12, one slide or cover per photo, with as many slides or covers as photos). It starts a new draft, so it is in Continue editing at once. **Several shops in a row** (1.6.0): Restock and Photo story also take several photos (up to 10) and make a story from each, one after the other: the first is in and saves in one tap; then **Next photo (2 of 3)** in the Save sheet brings in the next photo as a new story with the same words and look (one tap again), and Save again goes quiet so Next is the main button. Before saving, a computer shows **Skip to the next photo**. **Photos keep waiting** (1.8.0): the waiting photos are kept on the phone (IndexedDB `studio:queue:<brand>:<tool>`), so leaving the tool or a reload never drops them: Home shows **N photos still waiting** (tap to go on, ✕ to clear) and the tool page offers **Continue** / **Clear**. The "Photo 1 of 3 is in" note has a quiet **Skip** that brings in the next photo without saving; each story that was started stays a draft until it goes to the team. Tools opt in with `queue:true` in `TOOLS`.

**Templates** (1.0.0; bottom bar Home · Templates · Saved, also in search, or `index.html#templates`): every ready-made start in one place with a real preview, by kind: Restock, Stories, Posts, Highlights and Team (the team's own templates). One tap opens it ready to go: a tool template (brand kit `presets`: a tool, one of its styles and approved words) opens the tool with `?preset=<id>` as a new story; a Create template opens Create. Previews are `assets/thumbs/<brand>/tpl/<id>.jpg`, made with `node tests/tplthumbs.mjs [ids]` (real brand fonts; `--tool=restock` or `--create` for a group). The chosen kind is remembered on the phone (`studio:tplCat`). The page itself is `assets/templates.js/.css`; the hub hands it the list (`tplEntries()`). **Favourites**: tap the star next to a template's name; it shows in a Favourites section at the top of Templates (All) and first in its category. Tap again to take it out. **Recently used**: the last templates you opened, in one row under Favourites (4 on a phone, more on a computer). Both lists are kept on this device, per brand. Create's template picker has the same stars and a Recently used row (up to 3), shared with the Templates page.

**Reels in Templates:** the brand kit's reel templates (`reels` in `brand.js`, e.g. Winter cat) show under a **Reels** chip with a play mark and their length ("Video · 10 s"); a tap starts a new video from it in the Video editor (`tools/video.html?reel=<id>`). Favourites, Recently used and search work for them too. Previews: `node tests/reelthumbs.mjs`. To add a reel: `docs/reels.md`. Unfinished videos appear in **Continue editing** like the other tools (open it to save the MP4; **Save pictures** skips videos).

**Welcome tour** (1.0.0, `assets/welcome.js/.css`): the first time Studio opens on a phone that never used it, three short swipes show the main tools, pinning and search, and saving. Skip any time; it shows once (`studio:welcome`). **Settings → Welcome tour** (or `index.html?welcome=1`) shows it again. It never opens by itself under automation (`navigator.webdriver`), so tests open it on purpose.

**Settings** (the gear at the top of Home): Account / Sign in, Your tools (list or grid), Welcome tour, What's new, Install Studio, Experimental. Search finds Settings, Welcome tour and Templates too.

**Share into Studio** (Android, installed app): Studio is in the phone's Share list (`share_target` in `manifest.webmanifest`). Share one or more photos from the gallery and Studio opens its hub with a **pick a tool** sheet (same look as +): Restock, Photo story, Collage, Carousel or Highlight covers, with a preview of the photos (Collage takes up to 9, Carousel 10, Highlight covers 12; Restock and Photo story make a story from each, up to 10 in a row). The photos go through the service worker (`sw.js` keeps them in IndexedDB as `studio:shared` and opens `index.html?shared=<n>`), then to the tool exactly like Quick start. If the sheet is closed without a choice, Home shows **Your photo is in** under the search box until you tap **Pick** (the sheet again) or **✕** (forgets it). The photo tool used last on the phone has a small **Last time** tag in + and in this sheet (`studio:lastQuick`); the order never changes. iPhones don't offer websites in the Share list.

**Saved designs**: the hub shows the team's latest designs in a row with **See all**. See all (or **Saved** in the bottom bar, or `index.html#saved`) opens the Saved page: a picture grid, one row of chips: **All**, then the collections (tap again for All; the chosen one has a **⋯** with Rename collection and Delete collection, and Delete asks first), and **Sort** at the end of the row (a small sheet: Recent, Oldest, Name A to Z). Each card shows the picture at 3:4 for every design (posts and stories fill it; a Template badge in the bottom corner, ⋯ in the top one) with the name on one line (ellipsis; an Arabic name and its time start on the right, `dir="auto"`) and the time underneath. An empty Saved page says what to do and has **Make a design**; with fewer than 6 designs a tip sits under them. Tap a design to open it; press and hold, ⋯ or right-click for Open, Save the picture again, Duplicate, Rename, Collections, Select more and Delete (Delete asks first). Tap the picture at the top of that menu for **Peek**: the design full screen; swipe (or the arrows and ← → on a computer) to the designs next to it on the same page, **Open** to edit it, **More** for its menu. **Select** (top right) ticks several designs at once: tap designs to tick them (or **Select all**), then use the bar at the bottom: **Collection** (add them to an existing or new collection; their other collections stay), **Take out** (only while a collection is chosen: removes them from it) or **Delete** (asks first, names the count). ✕, Escape or Back stops selecting. On a computer the grid shows 7 designs a row. The Saved page is its own history entry, so Back returns home. **Save pictures** (1.18.0): in Select, tick designs (up to 20) and tap **Save pictures** in the bar to save the picture of every ticked design at full quality (2160×3840 stories, 2160×2700 posts, every slide), exactly as if you opened each one and tapped Save. A phone opens them in one share sheet; a computer gets one ZIP. It shows "Saving 2 of 5…" with Cancel, files are named after the designs, and templates are skipped.

**The look** (the "fresh look", default for everyone since 0.7.0): press and spring back, pages fade into each other (each page opts in with an inline `@view-transition` style), the home page settles in, soft shadows, a soft wash of colour at the top of the hub, one bold blue accent with a little light on top, sheets that slide up and land softly, a big tick and a short haptic when you save (Android buzz; iPhone iOS 18+ switch tick). The rules sit at the end of `studio.css`; `haptic`, `celebrate` in `studio.js`. Pop-ups, sheets, menus and the full-screen search lock the page behind them (`lockScroll` + `html:has(dialog[open])`).

**What's new** (✦ button at the top of the hub, and the version line at the bottom): the newest version as one big card, older versions in groups you open (0.1–0.5, 0.6–0.10 …), filter chips for Features, Fixes and Design, and before/after screenshots. A dot on the button means there is an update you haven't opened yet. The cards come from `studio-notes/changelog.md`; the version is `VERSION` in `assets/studio.js`. A big release can have a milestone banner (a `milestone:` line under its version in the changelog; 1.0.0 has one), and words in **bold** in the changelog show in bold.

Continue editing (hub) is a list of your unfinished stories. Every one is kept, several per tool. **Start fresh** starts a new one and keeps the old ones; a reload continues the same draft; tap ✕ to remove one (Studio asks first). Drafts saved to the team leave the list. Over 20 drafts of one tool shows a reminder; nothing is deleted on its own. **Save pictures** (1.23.0) at the top of Continue editing saves the picture of every unfinished story at full quality in one go (the same hidden-frame renderer as Save pictures in Saved, `?draft=<id>&render=1`; a render frame never writes a draft or touches the waiting photos): one share sheet on a phone, one ZIP on a computer; stories still missing a photo and Create designs are listed as skipped, and every draft stays in the list. The "New" labels on the hub are gone. A saved design you open never goes there: if you change it and leave, Studio asks to Update it or Discard the changes.

All tools lists tools in three groups (Stories, Posts and covers, Make anything), most used first. Photo tools that aren't pinned have the same camera button there as on Home (1.10.0), so Carousel and Highlight covers start from photos in 3 taps. Search (hub and Create) shares one Arabic normaliser (`Studio.normText`): harakat, letter forms and Arabic digits (٢٨ = 28) all match. The hub remembers per phone how you like Your tools (list / grid) and the Saved sort. The bottom bar (phone) has Home, Templates and Saved with matching Lucide icons (24px, 2px rounded lines; the tab you are on is filled); its highlight takes the tab's own size so icon and label sit in its middle. Tools that start from a photo (Restock, Photo story, Collage, Offer, Carousel, Highlight covers) have a camera: pick photos and they land in the tool.

**Type, spacing and dark mode** (0.7.1): the interface font is **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for Arabic and English (`@import` at the top of `studio.css`; designs keep the brand kit's fonts, which `studio.js` passes on as `--display` / `--brand-sans`). Three weights (400 text, 500 labels, 600 titles and buttons) and one spacing scale (4-8-12-16-24-32; sections 24px apart, a heading 12px above its content). Dark mode has layers: the page (`--bg`), cards (`--surface`), and sheets, menus and floating bars (`--raised`). `tests/contrast.mjs` checks text contrast (WCAG AA) and Arabic direction on every main screen in light and dark.

## Editing on the preview
- Tap any layer to select it. On a phone a slim bar opens (Edit, Size, Move, Colour, Font, More; all six fit on a 360px phone) and the preview stays big; it fades away while you drag.
- **Move** (layer bar): arrows nudge the layer a little at a time (hold to keep going, faster after a moment), **Centre** lines it up across the story and **Middle** from top to bottom; Rotate (slider, ±15°, Straight) is in Move too. Arrow keys still nudge on a computer. **Flip** (1.14.0) mirrors a layer left to right, so an arrow can point the other way (Rotate would turn it upside down); tap Flip again to put it back. Flip is for stickers, shapes and pictures; it is hidden for text, the logo and the cans (brand kit `noFlip:true`, so a can's label never reads backwards).
- Rotate text, buttons, the logo and assets with two fingers (twist), the Rotate slider or ±15° buttons; it settles on straight and right angles. With a layer selected, a two-finger pinch or twist anywhere on the preview acts on it. On a computer: [ and ] (Shift = 15°).
- Tap outside a pop-up to close it, or an empty spot beside the preview to close the open panel.
- Drag to move. Guide lines show when things line up and the safe zones (top 250px, bottom 300px) show while you drag. **Snap** (magnet + label, top bar) turns snapping to the guides on or off; it starts off and is tinted when on. Pinch or scroll to resize. Arrow keys nudge the selected item.
- **Add text** puts your own text on the story. **Add asset** adds the logo, the HD can cut-outs or a shape as a movable layer. **Layers → Put everything back where it started** resets the layout (Undo brings it back).
- **Layers → Element** adds any piece to any tool as a normal layer: Button, Map in bio (pill with pin), Place + pin, Flavour pills, Offer tile (top line + big line; type them on two lines), Dates line, Small line, Instagram handle, Logo. The words come from the brand kit.
- Every colour picker shows the same brand palette (White, Ink, Grey, Panel, Cream, Brown, Blob, Gold, Passion yellow, Passion berry) plus **Custom** (colour wheel or #hex). Custom colours you pick show after the brand swatches as **recent colours** (last 6, kept on this phone, in every tool and in Create). Every layer has **Opacity** (Colour tab).
- **Text layers** (Font tab): several lines (Enter = new line), align right / centre / left, line height, letter spacing (browsers that support it), soft shadow, and a background pill with its own colour.
- **Shapes** (Asset → Shapes): a new **Rounded panel** (the soft #F3F3F1 card behind cans) and **Blob**. Shapes have Fill (or none), Outline colour and width, Corners (panel, rectangle, soft box, line) and Opacity. Panel and Rectangle have free Width and Height.
- Added layers can be duplicated: **More → Duplicate**.
- **Copy style / Paste style** (1.7.0, a layer's **More** tab): Copy style keeps the look of the chosen layer (colour, font, align, line height, spacing, shadow, background pill, opacity; for shapes also outline and corners) on this phone (`studio:style`). **Paste style** shows once something is copied and puts that look on another layer, in this story or the next one, in any tool. Size, place and rotation stay. A button takes only its colour, font and opacity; Undo brings the old look back. **Paste on all text**: after Copy style, puts the copied look on every plain text layer in the story at once (one undo step); buttons and tags are left alone. **Text styles** (1.19.0): **Save this style** keeps the selected text's look (colour, font, align, line height, spacing, shadow, background pill, opacity) in a named list on this device (up to 8, auto-named "Style N", renamable from its ⋯ menu, which also has Use on all text and Remove). Tap a style to put it on the selected text or button. The list is hidden until the first style is saved.
- Photos: drag to move, pinch / slider to zoom. With two or more photos, hold one and drop it on another to swap them (in Collage a plain drag swaps). The same works with the boxes in the Photo sheet. With two or more photos the Photo sheet also has **Swap two** (tap it, then the two photos) and **Shuffle** (a random new order; Undo goes back).
- **Sticker** (Layers → Sticker, 1.12.0): a sheet of 14 drawn stickers (arrows, sparkles, starburst, star, heart, seal, scribble circle and underline, wavy line, speech bubble, shine lines, tick). Each is a normal layer (`{type:'asset', ref:'sticker:<id>'}`, `assets/stickers.js`): move, size, turn, recolour, opacity, duplicate; drawn as vectors, sharp in the 2160×3840 save; no words inside. Never rename a sticker id: saved designs point to it.
- **Photo look** (Photo sheet, for each photo): Original, Bright, Warm, Cool, Vivid, B&W or Soft, and a **Light** slider. Drawn as blend layers over the photo (`PHOTO_LOOKS` / `photoFx` in studio.js), so the saved 2160×3840 picture matches the preview on every phone; kept in the design (`recipe.photos[i].fx`). With several photos, **Use this look on every photo** copies it.
- iPhone HEIC photos work everywhere: a HEIC reader (`assets/vendor/heic-to.js`, LGPL) loads the first time one is picked; Safari opens them itself.

## Lab (experimental)
A small **Experimental** button at the end of the home page opens the lab after the passcode. The device remembers it; **Lock the lab** forgets it.
The passcode only hides the area from everyday use; it is not security. Nothing in the lab changes the studio tools or the team's saved designs.

**Board** (`lab/board.html`) is a free canvas editor, made for a computer (works on a phone/tablet for simple edits):
- **Moving around:** scroll or Space+drag (or the Hand tool) to move, pinch or Ctrl/⌘+scroll to zoom (2%–800%), mini map, Fit. Rulers along the top and left (Shift+R) measure from the frame you're in; drag from a ruler to add a guide, drag it back to remove it.
- **Tools** sit in a floating bar at the bottom: Move/Hand, Frame, Shapes (rectangle, ellipse, triangle, star, line, arrow), Text (and curved text), Draw (pen, highlighter, eraser), Upload, Brand kit, and More (barcode, QR code, mask, repeat, find and replace, replace a colour, versions). The small arrow on a tool, or tapping it again, shows its options. **Ctrl/⌘ K** finds any tool or action by typing.
- **Quick-edit bar** above whatever is selected: font, size, colour, bold, alignment; fill and outline; crop and photo looks; size; group, mask, duplicate, delete.
- **Real sizes for print:** pick px, mm, cm, in or pt (top bar). Print frames know their DPI (150/300/600), so a can label typed as 205 × 85 mm is exactly that. Bleed and a safe margin show as pink and green dashed lines; layout grids (columns, gap, margin) and guides snap.
- **Frames** of any size: social sizes, print sizes (A3–A6, Letter, business card, postcard, DL, labels, stickers) or custom. Copy a frame to another size with its content fitted.
- **Opening files:** photos (HEIC too); **SVG** files open as layers you can change (groups, shapes, paths, gradients, clip masks, text and embedded pictures, layer names; Canva text that was turned into shapes stays shapes); **PDF** pages (also .ai) become print frames at their real size, with the page artwork underneath and the text editable where the file has real text; board files.
- **Editing:** snapping to edges, centres, guides, grid columns and equal gaps; Alt to measure; Alt+drag to copy; groups; masks (a photo inside any shape); outline inside/centre/outside; corners one by one; layer blur; shadows; blend modes; gradients; text background boxes; saved text styles and colours; eyedropper on the design.
- **Photos:** crop/move inside the box, zoom, corners or a circle, fill the frame, make background, replace, 10 looks and 11 adjustments, spread across frames.
- **Combine shapes** (idea from [Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot)): union, subtract, intersect or exclude two or more shapes. They stay editable (double-click to move one); the bottom shape gives the colour; Release gives them back. Quick bar › Combine, right-click, or the Design panel.
- **Components** (also from Penpot): make anything a main component, add copies (Design panel, right-click, More › Components…), and every copy follows changes to the main. Words typed into a copy stay that copy's own; Reset words, Go to main, Detach. Copies can be sized on their own.
- **Flex layout (preview, off by default; 1.17.0, idea from Penpot):** a frame or group can stack its items in a column or a row with a gap, padding (sides / top and bottom), alignment and, for a fixed size, spread (start, centre, end, even). The stack re-flows when anything inside changes (a longer text pushes the rest along, even while typing); a group stack (or a frame set to "fits its contents") grows with what's inside. **Stack** (panel, right-click, Shift+A) puts several picked things in a new stack, guessing direction, order, gap and alignment from how they are placed. Drag inside a stack to reorder; locked things stay put; rows can run right to left; group stacks can have a box colour and round corners. Turn it on in the Design panel (nothing selected) → Previews → Flex layout, in the quick find (Ctrl K), or with `lab/board.html?flex=1` (`?flex=0` turns it off); it is kept on that device only. Boards made before look exactly the same.
- **Barcodes (EAN-13, UPC-A, check digit added for you) and QR codes** are made as sharp shapes.
- **Export:** PNG (lossless, see-through possible), JPG, WebP, **PDF** (every frame as a page at its exact size; print frames keep mm and DPI, with bleed and crop marks), **SVG** (layers kept, opens again in Board, Figma, Illustrator), one **ZIP** of many frames. Print PNG/JPG files carry their DPI so printers read the real size. Screen frames save at 2× (falling back to 1.5× then 1× only if the device can't make it that big).
- Layers panel with frames list (thumbnails, drag to reorder), hide, lock, rename, drag to reorder; folding Design / Effects / Export panels; right-click menu; copy/paste between boards; undo/redo; versions; find and replace; keyboard shortcuts (press ?).
- Boards autosave on this device (IndexedDB). Download a `.board` file (with its photos) to move a board. Uses Konva 9.3.22 (`assets/vendor/konva.min.js`, MIT), pdf.js 3.11.174 (`assets/vendor/pdfjs/`, Apache-2.0, loaded only when a PDF is opened) and qrcode-generator 1.4.4 (`assets/vendor/qrcode.js`, MIT).

## How it is built

Since 1.0.0 a tool can have styles that need no photo: `photosFor(o)` returns how many photos the current style uses (0 = no photo box, nothing to add before Save, and one-tap Save when opened fresh), `photoStyle` is the style to switch to when someone adds a photo anyway. `h.backdrop()` draws a soft-blurred copy of a photo (a tiny copy scaled up, so it also works on iPhones, which have no canvas blur filter). Automated checks of the owner's recurring issues live in `tests/taste.mjs`.

A brand kit can also set which approved line each tool starts with: `defaults:{offer:{head,pill},restock:{small}}` (otherwise the first line of each list); the pictures on the Style buttons take the kit's colours automatically (1.20.0). `tests/brandkit.mjs` runs Studio with a made-up brand (`tests/fixtures/brands/testbrand/`) and fails if a 1st Choice name, colour or font shows up.

### Add a brand
1. Copy `brands/1stchoice` to `brands/<new-id>` and change `brand.js` (logo file, fonts, colours, `dir`, phrases, samples).
2. Add `'<new-id>'` to `brands/brands.js`.
Tools never name a brand: they ask the kit for `phrases.headlines`, `colors.ink`, the logo, and so on.

### Add a tool
Copy a tool's `.html` and `.js`, give it a new `id`, and write its `draw(h, o)`:
`h.photo()`, `h.text(id,label,…)`, `h.pill()`, `h.pin()`, `h.logo()`, `h.tags()`, `h.block()` each create a layer people can move and edit. `h.isLight(hex)` tells whether a colour is light.
Then add it to `TOOLS` in `assets/studio.js` (the hub is built from that list), add a thumb in `assets/thumbs/<brand>/<id>.jpg` plus a 320px-wide `<id>-sm.jpg` for the hub,
sample photos in `brand.js`, and its two files to `SHELL` in `sw.js`.
Options: `size:{w,h}` for another canvas size, `pages:{min,max}` for slides, field types `chips`, `text`, `seg`, `toggle`, `multi`, `tiles`, `dates`, `place`, `range` (slider), `color` (palette picker; `null` = the style's own colour)
(`in:'style'` puts a field in the Style sheet).

### Speed
- Big brand pictures have a light copy (`prev:` in brand.js). Pickers, template previews and on-screen editing use it, so a page opens with about 1 MB instead of 40 MB. The HD file loads quietly behind it and replaces it; **Save always waits for the HD file**, so saved quality is the same. To add a light copy for a new asset: a ~1000px WebP of the PNG in `assets/prev/`, then `prev:'assets/prev/<name>.webp'` on its line.
- Create keeps its template previews on the phone (IndexedDB) after the first visit, so the start sheet fills at once. If you change how templates are drawn (create-templates.js / create-lib.js), change `TPL_LOOK` in create.js so phones redraw them.
- HEIC reader, PDF reader and QR maker load only the first time they're needed.

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so the site needs internet. Hosted with GitHub Pages (main branch, root).
