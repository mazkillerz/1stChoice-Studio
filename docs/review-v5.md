# Studio v5 · Phase 1 review

The eight tools, plus the engine they share, reviewed on a phone (390×844, touch) and a computer (1280×800). For each tool: what works, what's awkward, and what changed in Phase 1.

## Everywhere (engine)

**Works well:** the bottom bar opens sheets, and dragging, pinching or twisting layers feels natural. Undo and redo cover everything. Saves are full quality (2160×3840 PNG). Snapping is quiet unless you turn it on.

**Awkward (before Phase 1):**
- **Lost drafts:** each tool kept one unfinished story. **Start fresh** deleted it, and so did just starting to edit without tapping the banner. The owner lost a story this way.
- **Colours:** every picker offered a different short list (text: Auto, White, Dark, Gold; shapes: five colours), and none matched the colours used in the posts. Shapes started in gold.
- **Pieces stuck in one tool:** a button, place line, flavour pills or offer tile could only be used in the tool that has it.
- **Text:** one line only, with no alignment, spacing, shadow choice or background.
- **Shapes:** fill colour only, at a fixed aspect. There was no soft panel to go behind the cans.

**Changed:**
- **Drafts:** they are now a list per tool, and nothing is lost:
  - Start fresh begins a new draft and keeps the old ones.
  - A reload continues the same draft (its id is in the address).
  - ✕ in Continue editing asks first.
  - Old single drafts move into the list automatically.
  - Previews are stored with the photos, not in the small text store, so the list doesn't fill it up.
  - A reminder appears past 20 drafts of one tool.
- **Palette:** one brand palette, in the brand kit's order, in every picker: text, buttons, logo, elements, shapes, outlines, text pills, tool backgrounds and the Collage gap. **Custom…** gives a colour wheel or a #hex.
- **Elements:** a new **Element** menu in Layers adds Button, Map in bio, Place + pin, Flavour pills, Offer tile, Dates line, Small line, Instagram handle or Logo to any tool. Each one is a normal layer.
- **Text:** several lines, alignment, line height, letter spacing, soft shadow on/off, and a background pill with its own colour.
- **Every layer:** opacity, and added layers can be duplicated.
- **Logo:** can take any palette colour.
- **Shapes:**
  - New Rounded panel (#F3F3F1, free width and height) and Blob (#E5D5C0).
  - Fill or none, outline colour and width, corners, opacity.
  - Shapes now start in Ink instead of gold.
- **Zoom slider:** it hides for whole (uncropped) photos, where it did nothing.
- **Example photos:** tools with more photo boxes than example photos show the examples again, in turn.
- **Tapping turned photos:** turned photos are now tapped where they actually are. Before, the Collage Polaroids could not be swapped by touch.

## New place
- **Works:** a clear hierarchy (logo, photo window, headline, place with pin, button). "Save the frame only" is handy for re-use.
- **Awkward:** Black and White were the only grounds. On style C (photo on top) the logo can sit close to the photo with short headlines.
- **Changed:** a Background colour from the palette for every style. Text, pin and button follow the colour's lightness. The cream ring around the photo only shows on the Cream look.

## Photo story
- **Works:** text placement on a 3×3 picker, and the shade follows the words.
- **Awkward:** there was no white look at all.
- **Changed:** a new **White frame** style, and a Frame colour for the three frame styles.

## Cinematic bars
- **Works:** wide photos stay whole, and very tall ones fall back to a crop.
- **Awkward:** Black came first, but White is the main look.
- **Changed:** White first, then Cream and Black. A Background colour for the bars, with text and logo turning light on dark colours.

## Collage
- **Works:** whole photos are never cropped, and swapping is a hold and drag.
- **Awkward (before):**
  - Polaroids had a white frame the owner dislikes, and their photos couldn't be tapped.
  - Fixed gaps.
  - Only 2–4 photos and 16 layouts.
  - Rounded corners were on or off only.
- **Changed:**
  - 2–9 photos and 49 layouts: whole layouts for 2–4, plus grids, mosaics and bento for every count (2 rows/cols, 2×2, 3×3, Big + 2/3/4/5/7, L-shape, Bento, Hero + frame, Mosaic, Top hero + grid…). Each layout has a small drawing to pick from.
  - **Scattered prints** replaces Polaroids: no frame, small tilts, soft shadow. With wide photos the prints line up in one tilted column so they stay big. Old designs with Polaroids open as Scattered prints.
  - A **Gap** slider (0–60 px), a **Gap & background colour**, **Outer margin** on/off, and a **Corner radius** slider.
  - Gap 0 with no margin is full-bleed, edge to edge, and the logo sits on the photos.
  - Grid photos can be dragged and zoomed. Whole photos can't (nothing to crop).
- **Still awkward:**
  - Outer margin only affects the grids. Whole layouts already size themselves.
  - With margin on, grids start 40 px from the top, under Instagram's top bar. That's fine for photos, but don't put faces there.
  - Old designs that used the "Rounded corners" switch open with square corners. Set the Corner radius slider again.

## Two-photo split
- **Works:** the label on the cut reads well, and long words shrink to fit.
- **Changed:** a new **Cream label**, and a Label colour from the palette.

## Restock
- **Works:** the flavour pills come from the brand kit, and the "limited" small line is preset.
- **Changed:** White first (main look), then Cream and Full photo. A Background colour for White and Cream. The cream blob shows only on the Cream look, so White and Cream never mix.

## Offer
- **Works:** tiles write numbers in Arabic digits automatically, and the dates line is in Arabic.
- **Awkward:** there was no White look.
- **Changed:** a new **White** style (first), and a Background colour for White, Cream and Black. Tiles switch to gold on dark grounds.

## Carousel
- **Works:** slide types, panoramas across slides, and the filmstrip with long-press to reorder.
- **Changed:** White first. The Background colour is an "All slides" setting.
- **Still awkward:** the Style sheet is long. It mixes all-slides and this-slide settings (Phase 3 polish).

## Left for later phases
- **Layer bar:** on a phone the edit bar's six tabs scroll sideways, so "More" is half hidden. Phase 3 should check this.
- **Letter spacing:** needs a browser that supports it on canvas (Chrome, Edge, Safari 18+). The slider hides elsewhere.
- **Draft cap:** a soft reminder, not a hard limit, so a phone that runs out of room shows a message instead of dropping a draft.
