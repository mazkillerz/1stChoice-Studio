## 1. What changed (bugs found and fixed)

1. **Every Board control is at least 44px on a touch screen** (taste row 7; the old scan found 18–78 controls under 44px per screen at 390 px). One `@media (pointer:coarse)` block at the end of `lab/board.css`: top bar buttons, name box and Export 44px tall; panel tabs, section heads, folds, layer rows, fields, selects, segmented buttons, colour buttons, chips, text-style rows, links, menus, quick find rows, quick bar, zoom buttons, dock tools, pop-over swatches (6 a row in a wider pop-over), present bar. A computer (fine pointer) is unchanged.
2. **Shapes / Lines / Icons tiles** (backlog #47): 4 a row on touch (about 54px, were 43.8px at 5 a row); keyboard **emoji** 5 a row (about 45px, were 32px at 7 a row) and a bit bigger.
3. **Frame moves into More tools on a narrow phone** (touch, ≤ 420px): eight 44px tools don't fit at 360 px, so the dock keeps 7 (Move, Shape, Text, Draw, Upload, Brand kit, More) and More tools starts with **Frame** and **Frame sizes…** (the same menu as the dock's Frame options). Wider screens and computers keep Frame in the dock. Reason goes in the card (familiar button moved).
4. **Lock / Hide in Layers could be tapped by mistake** (bug): on a phone they were invisible (opacity 0 until mouse hover) but still tappable, so a tap at the end of a row hid or locked a layer with no visible button. On touch they now show and work on the chosen row and on a layer that is already locked or hidden; on other rows a tap there just selects the row.
5. **The quick bar floated on top of the open Layers panel** on a phone/tablet (old bug, the right panel was fine): panels that slide over the canvas (≤ 899 px) now sit above the quick bar, dock and zoom (`z-index:7`).
6. **A tap on the empty canvas didn't let go of the selection on a phone** (it started a pan and stopped there): a still tap (< 6px of movement) now clears the selection like a click does; a drag still pans and keeps it; a tap while typing ends the typing and lets go (`lab/board.js`, pan drag gets `tap`).
7. **Export showed "2× (sharp)" and "2×" side by side** for screen frames (the same choice twice): screen frames now offer 2× (sharp), 1×, 3×; print frames keep Print size, 1×, 2×, 3×. Output unchanged (2× = 2160 × 3840 for a story).
8. **"Use on all text" for text styles** (backlog #48): Assets › Text › a style's ⋯ now has **Use on all text** (like the tool pages): every unlocked, visible text and curved text in the frame you're on takes the style in one undo step; the note says "“Gold” is on 3 texts in this frame." with **Undo**. Hidden when the frame has no text. `UI.frameTexts()` + `tsMenu()` in `board-ui.js`.
9. **Weight names fit**: the Design panel's Weight box showed "Regula…" ("Semibold", "Extra bold" too); the word "Weight" is now a screen-reader label (and a tooltip), so the choice shows in full.
10. **Weights 400/500/600 only** in Board and the lab page (taste; backlog note from 1.17.0): every `font:700/800/900` and `font-weight:700/800` in `board.css` / `lab.css` / `lab.js` → 600, `<b>`/`<strong>` 600. Design text and previews keep their own weights (the "Subheading" sample stays 800 because it previews the real text).
11. **No keyboard shortcuts in phone menus** (touch, ≤ 620 px): "F", "Ctrl+F", "Ctrl K" badges are hidden in menus on a phone.

Tapped through on a phone (390 and 360, light + dark) with real touch drags: add a shape (tap with the tool), move it by dragging, undo twice, Text tool + typing, pan, Layers row tap, add a sticker, reload (all kept), Export dialog (fits). All fine apart from the bugs above. Computer at 1280×800: unchanged apart from items 7, 9, 10.
