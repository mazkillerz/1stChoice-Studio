### Words stay inside your story
type: fix
A headline or small line you type on two or three lines (Enter) no longer pushes the flavours, small line or button past the bottom of the story, off the design, or on top of the logo, in Restock, Photo story, Offer and Carousel. The photo makes room instead. A long button now shrinks to fit instead of running off both sides, and words on several lines are centred on a Highlight cover. Designs that already looked right are drawn exactly as before.

## How old designs stay identical (for lessons / the skill)
`h.measure` (in `makeHelpers`, studio.js) measures a string with a new line as ONE line, so every layout that pre-sizes with it got multi-line text wrong. Each tool now has a small `mLines` (true height for several lines; one line returns `h.measure` unchanged) and keeps two plans: the old one is used whenever the words really fit (inside 250..1620 on stories, on the slide and off the dots on Carousel, no overlap), the true-height one only where the old layout was broken. `tests/draw-edges.mjs` checks one-line designs and defaults are pixel-identical to the newest commit without `mLines`.
- **Simplify 1.50 idea:** fold `mLines` into `h.measure` itself (split on `\n`) and drop the six copies. It changes nothing for one-line text; check Create (`tools/create*`) uses of `h.measure` first.
- Lesson: shallow clones break the `*-styles` "compare with the old file" suites (they look for an older commit in git history): `git fetch --deepen=400 origin` first.

## Bugs found (backlog / lessons)
Fixed:
1. Restock (all six looks), Photo story (all eight), Offer, Carousel (cover, full, panorama, photo, two photos): a headline or small line on 2-3 lines overflowed: words past the 1620 safe line or off the canvas, or (Photo story) the headline over the small line and logo.
2. A long button (Restock, Offer, Carousel End slide) ran off both sides of the design (pill had no max width).
3. Offer White/Cream/Black: headline + photo + one tile + small line + button put the button at 1582..1658, below the safe line, with plain one-line words. Three tiles + dates too.
4. Photo story side row: a very long small line (60+ letters) ran under the logo; it now goes on its own row above the logo.
5. Collage "Big + small": a wide first photo and a tall second photo put the small print above the top of the story.
6. Highlight covers: a word or small word on several lines sat low (not centred); a 3-line small word left the circle.
7. Carousel cover/full: a 2-line headline + small line put the small line on the slide dots; Photo + words with 2-line words touched the slide's bottom edge.

Not fixed (noted):
- Offer tile with 8+ digit numbers fits only because the big line shrinks to 60px; very long tile words would still overflow the tile (numbers only today).
- Restock Full photo, Offer Full photo: the logo is drawn 230 wide but the gap below it is computed from 240 (`lgH`); kept so old designs stay identical.
- Highlight Product at 130 % pokes past the circle by design (it is the person's choice).
