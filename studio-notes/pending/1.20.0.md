## 2. Done

### 2.1 Brand kit plumbing (backlog #11): the engine no longer picks 1st Choice colours, fonts or words
Found by a static scan of `assets/` + `tools/` (vendor excluded) and by the new suite with a made-up brand:
- **Offer and Restock chose their start lines by matching 1st Choice's Arabic text** (`tools/offer.js` headline `'ما يتفوت'` and button `'اطلب الحين'`, `tools/restock.js` small line `/محدود/`). For any other brand the match failed and Offer silently fell back to whatever line was first in the list (the brand could not choose). Now a brand-kit key picks them: `defaults:{offer:{head,pill},restock:{small}}` in `brands/1stchoice/brand.js` (the same approved lines as before, so 1st Choice is unchanged), and a brand without the key gets the first line of each list.
- **Style swatch icons** (the little pictures on the Style buttons of Restock, Offer, Photo story, Highlight, Carousel, Split, New place, Cinematic) had 1st Choice's cream/ink/panel/yellow/gold hard-coded. `useBrand()` now publishes the kit's colours as CSS variables `--b-<key>` (one line), and the swatches use `var(--b-cream,…)` etc. Checked: 1st Choice computes the exact same colours (cream rgb(243,234,220), ink rgb(22,18,16)).
- **Fallback colours** in Create (`create-core.js`, `create-lib.js`, `create.js`), Highlight's disc, the sticker/shape thumbnails and `studio.css`'s `--display` were 1st Choice's (gold, cream, Lalezar…). They only apply when a kit lacks a key; now neutral (greys, `system-ui`). 1st Choice has every key, so its designs are unchanged.
- Left on purpose (allow-listed in the test, with a reason): `assets/config.js` teamEmail (the owner's Supabase login, deployment config); Studio's own interface tokens in `studio.css` `:root` (`--fg:#161210` etc.: the UI, not designs); the rainbow "Custom…" swatch.
- Saved designs: untouched. The recipe stores field values; a design that never stored the Offer headline/button or Restock small line gets the same default as before.
