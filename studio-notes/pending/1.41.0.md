## Bugs found and fixed
1. **A saved picture could use a stand-in font (the `batch-saved` pixel flake, root cause).** Reproduced: 4 copies of `batch-saved` at once failed "pixel identity" in 2 of 4 runs. Dumped both PNGs: the batch picture was identical in all 8 runs; the tool page's Save had the headline in the system Arabic font (thin) instead of Lalezar, and the photo sat lower because the text measured differently. Cause: `useBrand()` and `ensureFont()` add the Google Fonts `<link>` and call `document.fonts.load()` straight away; until the stylesheet is in there is no font face, so `load()` resolves at once with nothing and the page goes on as if the font were ready. Save never waited for fonts (the render frame waited for `document.fonts.ready`, which is why the batch was always right). On a slow phone network the same happens to the owner: the story is saved with the wrong font.
   Fix: `fontLink()` marks a font stylesheet when it has loaded or failed, `sheetIn()` waits for that; `useBrand()` and `ensureFont()` wait for the stylesheet inside their existing caps (4 s and 5 s, unchanged); new `fontsReady(families)` waits for every font stylesheet, then loads every face of the families the design draws with (brand display + body + any layer font), then `document.fonts.ready`, each step capped at 10 s so an offline phone still saves. Save and the render frame both call it (`saveFonts()` in `tool()`), so they wait the same way.
   Check: 12 runs of 4×`batch-saved` at once after the fix, 0 pixel-identity failures (was 3 of 8 before). New `tests/font-ready.mjs` (7 checks) holds the stylesheet 3 s and the font files 8 s and taps Save at once: same bytes as a Save with the fonts in; fails on the old code.
2. **Team save failure showed "Failed to fetch".** With the connection down, Save & add to team saved the picture, then showed the browser's raw "Failed to fetch" under the button and turned the team switch off; trying again meant finding the switch, and the next Save shared the picture a second time. Now: "Not saved to the team: no connection. Check it and try again.", the switch stays on, the button reads **Add to the team**, and one tap saves only to the team (no second picture); the old message clears when it works. Our own messages (e.g. "Not saved to the team: sign in first.") are kept as they are; the leave-page dialog's Update uses the same wording. New `tests/team-retry.mjs` (23 checks, phone light + dark + computer); 15 of them fail on the old code.
3. **`share.mjs` "address is clean after a share" (test-only).** Reproduced 3 of 12 under 6-way load. The app cleans the address as soon as the hub starts; the suite checked after a fixed 900 ms. Now it waits for the sheet / clean address, the service-worker controller, the hub's + button, and the tool's photos (fixed 600–2500 ms sleeps gone). 12 of 12 green at 6-way load after.
4. **`peek.mjs` "Peek fits the screen" (test-only).** On a computer dialogs pop in with a spring (`dlg-pop`, `--spring` overshoots past 100 %); under load the animation starts late, so the fixed 450 ms wait could measure mid-pop, bigger than the screen. `peekState()` now waits for Peek's animations to end (capped 3 s); Saved waits for its cards instead of 1400 ms. Did not reproduce in 6 old runs (it was always rare); the new run is 12 of 12 at 6-way load.

### Saved pictures always use the right font
type: fix
On a slow connection, a story saved straight after opening could come out with the headline in the phone's own font instead of the brand font.
