## The blank white preview (backlog, 30 Oct): cause found
`assets/reel.js` kept 3 full-size layer canvases (willReadFrequently) plus a scratch canvas and an ImageData for **every draw size it had ever seen**, forever: the sharp preview, the 540 px playing size, the 180×320 draft picture, the 360×640 team thumbnail, the 1080×1920 save estimate and export, the 36×64 timeline frames, and a new size on every preview resize (sheet open/close, the iPhone address bar). The `?debug=1` panel on a plain open already lists 4 sizes. On an iPhone that passes Safari's total canvas memory cap; past it a new canvas silently draws nothing. The reel paints its white background on the preview canvas first, then draws the scene through the dead layers, so what's left is **exactly a blank white preview**, with no error anywhere. Reproduced in Chromium by making canvases past a size draw nothing: `assets/whatsnew/1.44.0-blank-before.jpg` is the owner's report.

Fixes:
1. `reel.js`: layers and scratch canvases are kept for the newest sizes only, up to 4.2 M pixels in all (about two full frames) and at most 6 sizes; older ones are freed at once (`width=0`), not left to the garbage collector. Tiny sizes (timeline, draft picture) hardly count, so they don't push out the sharp preview. `reel.mem()` reports what is kept (shown in `?debug=1` as "canvases").
2. `reel.js`: a new layer canvas is checked (1 pixel); a see-through one throws "out of canvas memory" instead of drawing white.
3. `video.js`: after every new preview canvas size, one pixel is read back; see-through = dead canvas, so the editor tries half the size (down to 270 px wide), and when even that fails it shows "This phone ran out of memory for the preview. Close other tabs and apps, then tap Retry." Retry on a draw error now redraws (no reload of the reel). A null 2D context gets the same message. Canvases with another site's pictures (can't be read back) are trusted, as before.
4. `reel-export.js`: the two 1080×1920 export frames are freed when a save ends (both paths).
Still worth checking on the owner's iPhone with `?debug=1` once merged (the "canvases" row shows the sizes kept).

## Other bugs found and fixed
5. **Mix (and every sheet) can grow on a phone**: drag the sheet's top edge (a 120×44 handle with a pill) or its title row up for more room, down for a bigger preview (210 px up to the height that leaves the preview ~180 px). Remembered on this device (`studio:video:sheetH`, localStorage, per-viewer). Arrow keys on the handle (role=separator). While dragging only the CSS size changes; the canvas is resized once on release (no new canvas sizes per frame). No handle on a computer (side panel). Mix at 390×844: 2 sounds in view → 6.
6. **Recordings with no length**: a WebM from a screen/phone recorder reports `duration = Infinity` until its end is read; it went into the recipe as `out: Infinity` (an endless timeline). `StudioMedia` now seeks to the end once to learn the real length.
7. **`?debug=1` panel covered the bottom bar on a phone** (bottom-left, took the taps): now under the header over the preview, taps go through it (only Close is live), so the owner can keep editing while screenshotting it.

Checked, no bug: every video sheet at 390 and 360 px (light and dark): no sideways scroll, nothing under 44 px except labels and the 28 px timeline lanes (by design); the 42 px colour input sits inside a 44 px circle; computer layout fine.

## What's New cards (changelog format)

### Video editor: no more blank white preview
type: fix
On some phones the video preview could turn blank white. The editor kept a full-size copy of the picture for every size it had shown, and the phone ran out of memory for pictures. Now it keeps only what it needs. If a phone still runs short, the preview steps down to a smaller size, and if even that fails it says so with a Retry button.

type: design
On a phone, drag the top edge of the Mix sheet (or any sheet) up to see more sounds at once, and down again for a bigger preview. Studio remembers the height you like. Also: screen recordings now get their real length when you add them, and the ?debug=1 panel no longer covers the buttons.

![after](assets/whatsnew/1.44.0-mix-after.jpg)

## Backlog / lessons / taste notes
- Backlog line 9 (blank white preview): cause found and fixed (canvas memory, above); left: confirm on the owner's iPhone with `?debug=1`. "Mix sheet short on a phone (213 px): letting it grow taller when dragged up": done.
- Backlog: AAC export on a real iPhone and the motion-blur speed on a mid-range phone are still untested (no device here).
- Lesson: on iPhone Safari a canvas past the total memory cap does not throw; it silently draws nothing. Anything that makes canvases per size (layers, scratch, thumbnails) needs a cap and must free with `width=0`. A blank result is detectable: read one pixel after a draw that paints the whole frame.
- Lesson (tests): a fast synthetic touch drag starts a fling, and Chrome then ignores the next tap (tap that stops a fling). Hold ~250 ms before lifting in drag helpers.
- Lesson (tests): `import 'playwright'` in tests needs a `node_modules` above the repo in this sandbox (`ln -s /opt/node22/lib/node_modules /home/user/node_modules`); scratch scripts can use `createRequire`.
