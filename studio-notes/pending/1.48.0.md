## Bugs found (all fixed)
1. **Share sheet closed without a choice: the photo vanished from Home until a reload.** `openShared()` never called `sharedWaiting()` on close (only the `#sharedGo` path did), so after Share → Studio → ✕ the "Your photo is in" row only came back on the next visit. Now the sheet's close brings the row back at once (not after a choice, and not when the hand-over failed and you closed). `#sharedGo` no longer adds its own close listener (no double row) and frees its preview URL.
2. **The tool's "unfinished story" banner opened the wrong story.** The banner counts only drafts with something in them (`real`: a photo, or a recipe that differs from the fresh tool), but **Continue** opened `listDrafts()[0]`, the newest draft of any kind. An edit undone and then left writes a blank draft, so Continue opened a blank story while the one with the photo stayed hidden. Both now use `realDraft(x)` (a hoisted function next to the start-up code).
3. **Home was stale after the phone's Back button or another tab.** Continue editing and the "N photos still waiting" rows / "N waiting" tags were read once at load. A page restored from the back/forward cache (Back from a tool on a phone) kept showing a finished row and missed the story just left. Now `refreshLocal()` re-reads both on `pageshow` with `persisted` and when the page becomes visible again after being hidden. Continue editing repaints only when the list changed; previews are cached per edit time (`tk(x)` = key@at), so an edited story shows its new picture. `queueWaiting()` now reads first and swaps its rows in once (sequence guard), so two reads never double the rows, and it frees the old preview URLs.
4. **Quick start: leaving within 800 ms lost the photo from the draft.** The draft record is written on leave (1.13.0) but its photos followed after the page was gone, so the story reopened with the example photo (seen in a test that went Home right after a Quick start; on a phone: pick a photo, back out at once). Quick start now writes the draft (photos first) straight away instead of waiting for the 800 ms timer.

## The improvement (saves a trip to Home)
**Photos still waiting join a new Quick start.** In Restock / Photo story, if photos from an earlier row are still waiting and you start again from + / a row camera / search / a share, they now go after the new photo(s) in the same row: "Photo 1 of 3 is in · And 2 photos from before", Skip / Next photo reaches them right there. Before, one new photo left them hidden until you went back to Home and tapped the waiting row (2 extra taps + a page), and a new row of several photos silently replaced them. The old row's unsaved story (if any) stays in Continue editing as before. Home's count stays right (2 waiting, not 4).

## Backlog / lessons / taste notes
- Lesson: a draft record written on leave is only half the story: its photos are written after it, and a page that is gone stops the write. Anything that adds photos (Quick start, now fixed) should write the draft at once rather than on the 800 ms timer. Adding a photo by hand still uses the timer (the photo write starts within 800 ms; leaving faster than that still keeps the record without the photo): backlog idea, write at once whenever a photo is added (`setPhoto`), small.
- Lesson: Home read things once at load; with the back/forward cache on phones a page can come back hours later. Anything Home shows from this device's storage should be re-read on `pageshow` (`persisted`).
- Lesson: a test that compares sample photos by size proves nothing (all brand samples are 1200×800); compare the file name (`S.photos[i].file.name`; drafts and the queue keep the File).
- Seen, not changed: two tabs on the same draft (`?draft=X` open twice) both write it, last write wins; rare, no data loss beyond the other tab's last edits.
- Seen, not changed: Home can still show a Continue editing card and a waiting row for the same Restock job (backlog note from 1.10.0); the card for the queue's current story opens it with the row restored, so both work.
