## What changed (bugs fixed)
1. **Words stay on the page.** Words that sit right on a page keep their place while they change in the Words sheet or with Font → Size: centred words grow from their centre, right-aligned from their right edge, left-aligned from their left. When they get wider than the room they have (page width minus 6% margins, measured from that anchor), they wrap onto more lines instead of running off the page; they un-wrap when they get shorter again (`fitW` attr = the wrap width; a box someone sized by its side handles keeps its width). Rotated or scaled texts are left alone. Saved/drafted designs are unchanged until their words are edited.
2. **Chat bubbles keep their side.** `CR.layout()` re-centred every smart group after a change, which undid the bubble's own "stay pinned to your side" code: a long message grew both ways and ran past the page edge (to x 1147 of 1080), and your own bubble drifted off the left margin. Bubbles now keep their side and their top (`create-lib.js` `layout()`, one condition).
3. **360px: all five sizes fit** (start sheet and Templates sheet). Highlight was cut off at the right edge in a hidden sideways scroller (5 × 62px + 4 × 8px gaps > 328px). At ≤380px the row uses 6px gaps and 2px side padding; 390px and up unchanged.
4. **Team templates never stick on "Looking…".** If the list fails, the picker says "Team templates could not load. Check the connection." with **Try again**; if it takes over 12 s, "Team templates are taking too long…" with Try again (before: a failure had no retry and a hung request showed Looking… forever).
5. **Targets ≥ 44px:** Add sheet jump chips 38 → 44px, Layers Hide / Lock 40 → 44px, colour swatches keep their 40px look with a 44px `::after` hit area (8px gaps, so hit areas never overlap).
6. **Save sheet:** no empty 48px gap under "Full quality" before you save (the empty status line is hidden until it has something to say).
7. **Dead CSS:** `.cx-sep` (no element uses it, 1.45.0's finding) removed from `tools/create.css`; a copy is in `_archive/unused css (2026-11-05)/create-cx-sep.css`.

The improvement for this area is #1 (+ #2): typing a long Arabic headline no longer needs a trip to Size or a drag to bring it back on the page. Taps: edit a long headline so it fits = Words + type + Done (3) instead of Words + type + Done + Font + drag size + Done + drag the text back to the middle (≈ 7).
