### Done (one What's New card each, below)
1. **Computer: the Save card stops covering the steps.** It used to stick to the bottom of the screen, about 470px tall on an 800px screen. Now it sits at the end of the steps, and while its button is out of sight a slim dock (76px) at the bottom shows the same Save button with the same words (it copies the card's button: label, disabled, ghost). One click brings the card up and saves, so the progress and the tick show. The dock hides when the card is in view and never shows on a phone. Code: `#saveDock` before `#sh-save`, one block after `$('save').addEventListener` in `tool()` (MutationObserver + IntersectionObserver), CSS `.save-dock` replacing `.save-sheet{position:sticky}` in the ≥821px block.

3. **Text buttons, not underlined links.** `.link` (Reset photo, Use this look on every photo, More options, Only save to the team, Save this slide only, Save the frame only, Put everything back…, Cancel, Close, search's Clear) is blue text with a soft press shape, no underline; Reset photo and More options keep their left edge (negative margin). Also the hub footer "Studio 1.x · What's new" (muted, no underline) and the toast's Undo (a small pill).
