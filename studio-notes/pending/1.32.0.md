## 1. Done
1. **Remove saved stories from Continue editing (#41).** After Save pictures in Continue editing, the Saved! dialog shows a blue
   text button "Remove these N from Continue editing" ("Remove it …" for one). Tap → the same dialog asks "Remove these N stories
   from Continue editing?" (note: pictures are saved, the unfinished stories and their photos are deleted from this phone/device,
   can't be undone) with **Keep them** (grey) and **Remove** (red). Never automatic. Only the stories whose pictures were actually
   drawn are offered: skipped ones (Create drafts, videos, "has no photo yet", "could not be opened") are never counted. A story
   edited in another tab after its picture was made is kept (its `at` changed). Not shown on "Ready!" (share sheet closed, nothing
   saved yet) nor on "Nothing saved"; Saved → Select → Save pictures never shows it (no `offer` passed).
   - `assets/render.js`: `saveDesigns(list,{…,offer})`, `offer={label(n),title(n),note(n),yes,no(n),run(items)}`; `saved[]` holds
     the items that produced files; the offer shows from `finish()` only. Wording lives in `home()` (engine, brand-neutral).
   - `home()`: Continue items carry `key` and `at`; `run()` → `removeDraft()` (the same as ✕) + `paintCont()` + a toast
     "2 stories removed from Continue editing." / "Nothing removed: those stories changed since."
2. **Saved search: day words.** `findHit()` also understands "today", "yesterday" and "this week" (today and the 6 days before),
   matched against `lastUse(d)` (the date the cards and the Recent sort use). Combine with words ("restock today"). A design whose
   name/tool/collection has the word still matches by name ("Today's offer"). "week" alone is a plain word. The no-match note now
   says "Try part of the name, a tool such as Restock, a collection, or a day: today, yesterday, this week."
   - **Arabic:** the engine stays brand-neutral; a brand kit may add its own day words: `findDates:{today:[…],yesterday:[…],week:[…]}`
     in `brand.js`, folded with `normText` (harakat, hamza forms). `brands/1stchoice/brand.js` is **not changed** (outside my scope
     and the words need approval; see section 6). Tested with words injected into brand.js by the suite only.
3. **New place and Cinematic bars: camera in All tools (#37).** `quick:` added to both `TOOLS` rows ("A photo of the new place",
   "A wide photo, shown whole"), no `qi`, so like Offer: a camera in All tools when not pinned (and on their Home row if pinned);
   not in the + menu, the share-target sheet, the welcome or a new device's default pins. New place / Cinematic from a photo:
   3 taps (All tools, camera, Save) instead of 4 (All tools, tool, Add your photo, Save).

## 3. What's New cards (changelog format)

```
### Clear Continue editing after saving
type: feature
After Save pictures in Continue editing, the Saved! message now offers "Remove these 3 from Continue editing". Tap it, then Remove, and the stories you just saved leave the list (their photos too); Keep them leaves everything as it was. It never happens by itself, and a story that was skipped (like a Create design) is never removed. Before: tap ✕ and Remove on every story; now: 2 taps for all of them.

![after](assets/whatsnew/1.32.0-continue-after.jpg)

type: feature
Search in Saved now understands days: type "today", "yesterday" or "this week" (the last 7 days) and only the designs from then stay on the page. Mix it with a word, like "restock today". Names still count: "Today's offer" is found by its name too.

![after](assets/whatsnew/1.32.0-find-after.jpg)
```
