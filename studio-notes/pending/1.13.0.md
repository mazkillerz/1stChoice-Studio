```
### A story you leave at once is kept
type: fix
Studio keeps every unfinished story in **Continue editing**, but it waited a moment (0.8 s) before writing it. Leaving the page, or tapping **Skip** to the next photo, inside that moment could lose the story or its last change. Now leaving, hiding the app or skipping writes it straight away.

### The right word on a computer
type: fix
When a computer runs short of memory while saving, Studio steps the picture down a little and tells you. It used to say "This phone was low on memory" on a computer too; it now says "device" there (a phone still says "phone").
```

## Root cause of the `queue.mjs` "no leftover unfinished stories" flake (backlog UX audit, top item)

- Reproduced: 6 of 8 queue runs failed with 3 other suites running at once (`queue`, `queue-keep`, `v5` alongside).
- The leftover was **not** a queued story. Logging draft create/write/clear showed it was the test's earlier **one-photo** step: Quick start → `flush()` creates a draft (id) and starts the 800 ms draft timer; the test then navigates away at once. Without load the page unloaded before the timer fired, so the draft was never written (silently lost); under load the timer fired first, so a legitimate unfinished story stayed, and the final count saw it.
- So the race was "pending draft write vs leaving the page", in real use too: a photo picked and left within 0.8 s was not in Continue editing; **Skip** (`nextInQueue()`) also dropped the skipped story's pending write.
- Fix (engine, `tool()` drafts): the timer body is now `writeDraft(now)`; `pagehide` and `visibilitychange→hidden` call `draftNow()` (flush the 450 ms undo commit, then write the pending draft with the record first, photos after); `nextInQueue()` writes the pending draft before starting the next story instead of dropping it; a sequence number stops an older, slower write overwriting a newer record.
- Test: `queue.mjs` now checks "the one-photo story, left at once, is kept in Continue editing" (fails 2/2 on the old engine, alone, deterministically) and its leftover check counts only drafts other than that one.
- Proof: 8 loaded rounds (queue + queue-keep + v5 + quicksave at once) all green; see the session report for counts.

## Lessons

- A flaky check that "never fails alone" may be a **timing-dependent outcome**, not a slow one: here load made a pending write *succeed*. Log the create/write/clear of the thing left over (with a stack) before touching timeouts; the printed key's id-timestamp vs its `at` (≈ 800 ms) pointed straight at the draft timer.
- Prove a race fix with a check that fails deterministically on the old code (here: leave at once, expect the draft), not only with N green loaded runs.
